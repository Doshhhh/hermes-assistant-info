PERSONAL GOOGLE WORKSPACE INTEGRATION

Hermes Assistant

Hermes Assistant is a privately operated personal assistant built on Hermes Agent. It helps its owner review email, manage a calendar, and prepare personal summaries through a private assistant interface.

Google account access

Google OAuth connects the owner's Gmail and Google Calendar accounts. Gmail access supports reading and organizing messages, drafting and sending replies. Calendar access supports reading and managing events. Sending email or changing calendar events requires the owner's instruction or confirmation.

This integration is for the operator's personal use. It is not a public sign-up service and is not affiliated with Google or the Hermes Agent maintainers.
