Hermes Assistant

Privacy policy

Effective October 16, 2026

Hermes Assistant is a personal integration operated by the GitHub account Doshhhh for the operator's own Google account. This policy describes the integration's handling of Google account data.

Data accessed and purpose

With OAuth consent, the assistant can access Gmail account information, message bodies, headers, labels and message metadata, and Google Calendar events and calendar metadata. It uses this data to answer the owner's requests, prepare summaries, organize email and manage events. Sending messages and changing calendar events require the owner's instruction or confirmation. The integration requests Gmail and Calendar access; it does not request Drive, Contacts, Sheets or Docs access.

Storage and retention

OAuth credentials are stored in private files in the assistant's profile on the operator-controlled server. Relevant email or calendar information may appear in private conversation histories, notes or backups when used in a task. Such records remain until the operator deletes them; retained backups may contain earlier copies. Google mailbox and calendar originals remain with Google unless the owner requests changes.

Processing and sharing

Relevant portions of requested Google data may be sent to the AI model provider configured by the operator to produce an answer. That provider's processing and retention terms also apply. The operator should consider those terms before requesting processing of sensitive information. The integration does not sell Google account data, use it for advertising, or intentionally publish it. It does not use Google account data to train general-purpose AI models.

Security

OAuth avoids storing the Google account password. Credentials are kept out of the public website and source repository and are restricted to the assistant profile. Access to the server, conversations and backups is controlled by the operator. No storage or transmission method can be guaranteed completely secure.

Revocation and deletion

The owner can revoke access through Google Account connections. Revocation stops future authorized access but does not remove previously saved records. The owner can also remove local OAuth credentials and delete relevant assistant histories, notes and backups on their server.

This website

These static pages contain no analytics scripts, sign-in form or Google account data. Hosting is provided by GitHub Pages, which may process visitor connection information under GitHub's privacy statement.

Contact and changes

Contact the operator through this repository's issues. Do not post emails, calendar details, credentials or other private information there. Updates to this policy will be published on this page.
